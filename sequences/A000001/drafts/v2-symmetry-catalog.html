OEIS · A000001

Catalog of symmetries

Shelf n — every distinct object that can be rotated or flipped exactly n ways so it looks the same as before. How many objects are on the shelf is the number from the sequence.

Why there are no others. Any movement, repeated, returns the object after a number of steps that evenly divides n. Above the shelf are the divisors of its number; the pink ones (other than 1 and n) are the allowed short returns, and objects with flips are built from them. No pink ones — only a pinwheel.
