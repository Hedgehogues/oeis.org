OEIS · A000019

No room for a smaller piece

? Problem

A group is primitive if it can't split its points into smaller equal chunks it only ever shuffles whole. Degree 4 and degree 5 — which one splits?

↓

1 A chunk size has to divide the point count

A chunk size must divide the point count. Compare the divisors of 4 and of 5:

5 is prime — only divisors 1 and 5, so no candidate chunk size at all. Degree 4 has a real one: size 2.

✓ Solution

– primitive groups of degree 5

Prime degree forces the rotation group itself to be primitive. The full count also includes larger groups, each checked the same way.
