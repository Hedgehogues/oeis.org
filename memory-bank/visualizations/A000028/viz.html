OEIS · A000028

Counting 1-bits in the exponents

? Problem

Some whole numbers are marked, some aren't. What sorts them?

↓

1 Write each exponent in binary, count its 1-bits

1a

24 = 2³ × 3¹ — the 1-bit counts add up to an odd total, so 24 is marked:

↓

2 An even total leaves it out

2a

12 = 2² × 3¹ — same shape, different exponents, an even total:

↓

✓ Solution

A number is marked exactly when its exponents' 1-bit counts add up to an odd total — checked for every number, not just the two worked here:

✓ verified

factor m → each exponent in binary → count its 1-bits → add them up → odd or even
